## Brand & Style

The design system powers the identity of a premier collegiate cultural and sports festival. It captures the tension between majestic tradition and youthful athletic kineticism. The brand personality is electric, regal, celebratory, and celebratory yet operationally sharp.

### Design Movement
The visual language merges **Modern Editorial Glassmorphism** with **Neo-Festive Opulence**:
- Atmospheric nocturnal backdrops soaked in deep indigo and spectral magenta glows.
- Luminous glass tiers that elevate content with crystalline clarity against complex celebratory art.
- Subtle geometric Indian folk and mandala watermarks acting as structural ornamentation rather than decorative clutter.
- High-velocity sports accents cutting through royal palettes with sharp cyan vector vectors.

### Emotional Target
The interface must evoke euphoric anticipation, competitive pride, and effortless discovery for thousands of attendees, performers, and athletes navigating high-density schedules and registrations.

## Colors

The palette operates on a dual-canvas philosophy: a nocturnal default canvas for festival grandeur and hero moments, complemented by high-legibility warm ivory surfaces for dense analytical readouts (rulebooks, brackets, and ticketing tables).

### Core Spectral Tiers
- **Midnight Indigo Base (`#140524`, `#1E0B36`, `#2B1055`)**: Serves as the primary night sky canvas, evoking regal depth and stage ambiance.
- **Electric Magenta (`#801B68`, `#A82386`)**: Directs the cultural vibrancy; used for headliner spotlights, live indicators, and primary call-to-actions.
- **Imperial Gold (`#E5A93C`, `#F3C766`, `#FFDF85`)**: Celebrates victory, cultural prestige, awards, and tier-one headliners.
- **Kinetic Cyan (`#0284C7`, `#0EA5E9`, `#38BDF8`)**: Dedicated entirely to sports events, track updates, tournament brackets, and live scores to maintain contextual instant recognition.
- **Warm Ivory Neutral (`#FDFBF7`, `#FAF6EE`, `#F3EFE6`)**: Utilized in bounded daylight cards and documentation overlays to guarantee zero eye fatigue during rule reading.
- **Surface Borders (`rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.2)` & `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`)**: Ultra-thin luminous hairpins framing interactive surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances dramatic editorial authority with razor-sharp computational legibility.

- **Display & Large Headlines (Playfair Display)**: Used exclusively for festival crowns, category anchors, and high-impact hero claims. Its high contrast evokes majestic traditional celebrations.
- **Section Headers & Metric Accents (Outfit)**: A crisp, geometric sans providing modern contrast to the serif headings. Used for category tags, dates, prize amounts, and sports scoreboard numbers.
- **Body & Interactive Layers (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly optimized for micro-reading, rule sheets, event itineraries, and modal notices. Ensures rapid absorption under intense outdoor daylight or dark festival conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform follows a responsive 12-column grid desktop framework shifting to an 8-column tablet and 4-column mobile arrangement.

### Viewport Breakpoints & Margins
- **Desktop (≥1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1360px`, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid, fluid width, `1.5rem` (24px) margins, `1rem` (16px) gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` (16px) margins, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to generous macro-spacing (`4rem` to `6rem`) to allow atmospheric glow elements and ornamental mandalas to breathe without crowding typography. Micro-spacing within cards and lists anchors strictly to the `4px` base increment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous chromatic backdrops rather than standard grey drop shadows. Surfaces appear as suspended crystalline panes etched with gold or cyan light.

### Depth Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0)**: `#140524` with deep radial chromatic blurs (`#2B1055` at 40% blur radius `120px` and `#801B68` at 20% blur radius `160px`).
- **Surface Low (Level 1 - Interactive Tiles, Filters)**: Background `rgba(35, 18, 61, 0.65)`, backdrop blur `12px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Mid (Level 2 - Cards, Persistent Navigation)**: Background `rgba(43, 16, 85, 0.55)`, backdrop blur `20px`, border `1px solid rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.25)`, shadow `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(10, 2, 20, 0.6)`.
- **Surface Floating (Level 3 - Overlays, Drawers, Modals)**: Background `rgba(24, 9, 42, 0.92)`, backdrop blur `28px`, border `1px solid rgba(243, 199, 102, 0.45)`, shadow `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px rgba(168, 35, 134, 0.2)`.

### Mandala & Pattern Treatment
Subtle vector mandala rings are rendered at 3% to 6% opacity in gold or pure white, positioned as bleed ornaments behind hero headlines and card backgrounds. They never intersect high-density body typography.

## Shapes

The geometric framework couples modern rounded architecture with sharp edge accents to harmonize festival elegance with competitive sports tension.

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Base radius of `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) creates an inviting modern aperture.
- **Nested Controls & Inner Panels**: `0.5rem` (`rounded`) maintains systematic scale within parent containers.
- **Chips, Countdowns & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to visually separate operational data pills from structural content cards.
- **Hero Banners & Category Portals**: Deep luxury corners (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`) combined with 1px inset glowing perimeter lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Festive (Cultural)**: Solid gradient from `#A82386` to `#801B68` with a 1px gold border overlay (`rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.4)`). White text, `Outfit` font, uppercase tracking. Glows magenta on hover.
- **Sports Action (Athletic)**: Solid `#0EA5E9` with electric hover shift to `#38BDF8`. Text `#140524` in `Outfit` Bold.
- **Secondary Glass**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text `#FAF6EE`. Hover shifts to `rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.15)` with gold stroke.

### Event Cards
- Built on Surface Mid specs with a 16:9 media frame topping a structured glass lower block.
- Feature dual-badge headers: Category (Cultural: Magenta/Gold, Sports: Cyan/White) on the left, Registration Status Pill on the right.
- Prize pool highlighted in `Playfair Display` Gold (`#F3C766`).
- Subtle mandala line art vector stamped in the bottom corner at 5% opacity, shifting to 12% on card hover.

### Countdown Chips
- Pill enclosures with dark indigo glass core (`#1E0B36`) and dual-tone borders.
- Split-cell time counters showing Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds with `Outfit` semi-bold numbers and `label-sm` tracking labels below.
- Pulsing red/gold live beacon dot when under 24 hours.

### Announcement Ticker
- Edge-to-edge ribbon pinned below the main navigation.
- Deep violet backdrop (`#18092A`) framed by dual 1px gold hairline borders.
- Uses kinetic marquee animation featuring flashing sports results, headline artist reveals, and schedule shifts with gold lightning/star glyph dividers.

### Tabs
- Contained segment controllers with a blurred pill chassis (`rgba(20, 5, 36, 0.7)`).
- Active tab floats with smooth transition, coated in metallic gold gradient for cultural filters or vibrant cyan for sports divisions.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form inputs feature dark midnight fills (`rgba(20, 5, 36, 0.8)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Focus triggers a 2px expansion glow in Imperial Gold (`#E5A93C`) or Cyan.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons feature custom jewel styling: hexagonal or rounded glyphs filled with magenta/cyan accents upon selection.

### Responsive Navigation
- Desktop: Suspended floating glass bar (`rgba(24, 9, 42, 0.8)`) with frosted blur, pinned 16px below the top viewport, framed by a delicate gold gradient bottom rim.
- Mobile: Bottom thumb bar for core routing (Schedule, Events, Leaderboard, Pass) paired with a full-screen festive curtain drawer featuring geometric mandala backdrops.
