THE FESTIVAL

More Than an Event.
A Celebration of Talent.

COLORIDO 2K26 is a premier college-level Cultural and Sports Festival that brings together creativity, performance, competition and sporting spirit. It serves as an open stage where students push boundaries, discover lifelong passions, and celebrate youth.

CULTURE

Celebrate creativity, expression and living traditions through music, fine arts, drama and dance.

SPORTS

Compete with energy, discipline, resilience, and unyielding team sportsmanship.

COMMUNITY

Bring students together through shared triumphs, unforgettable nights, and collaborative spirit.
