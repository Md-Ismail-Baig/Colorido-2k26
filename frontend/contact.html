Frequently asked questions regarding registration, guidelines, and festival operations

Open the Events page (/events), select any Cultural or Sports event to view guidelines, and click 'Register Now'. Complete the multi-step participant registration wizard and download your official confirmation pass.
Detailed rules, eligibility, round schedules, and judging criteria are available directly in each event's details panel inside the Events listing workspace.
Enter your unique generated Registration ID (e.g., CLR26-XXXXXX) or Student Roll Number in the Registration portal status lookup tool.
