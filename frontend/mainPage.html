02 / ABOUT COLORIDO

Where Heritage Meets Athletic Excellence

COLORIDO 2K26 is a premier inter-collegiate Cultural & Sports Festival, curated to celebrate creative prowess, ethnic vibrancy, and athletic tenacity under one unified platform.

Rooted in our commitment to holistic collegiate life, COLORIDO serves as a grand confluence where time-honored traditional performing arts harmonize with the competitive adrenaline of the sports arena.

Whether executing graceful classical choreographies, energizing crowds during dynamic battle-of-the-bands, or dominating the court in basketball and volleyball championships, every participant is empowered to compete with honor, mutual respect, and unbound enthusiasm.
